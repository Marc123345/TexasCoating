Southern Oklahoma only — and that is the point. The Red River is a state line, not a market boundary: Ardmore is 55 road miles from the Denison centre line, closer than Paris, TX. What is deliberately NOT here is the rest of the state. Oklahoma City is 160 road miles and Tulsa 206 — real markets, but a day each way against local contractors, which is a different business from the thin-competition work this territory is built on. Lawton and Duncan sit 48 and 51 miles from Wichita Falls, so they belong to that zone if it goes live, not to this one.
