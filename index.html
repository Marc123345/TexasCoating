Oklahoma is new territory, not an extension. The Red River is a state line, not a market boundary — the hail belt, the ag buildings and the metal industrial stock all carry straight over it. Tulsa and Oklahoma City are the two metro anchors; everything between them is the same thin-spread commercial roof stock the West Texas zones are built on.
